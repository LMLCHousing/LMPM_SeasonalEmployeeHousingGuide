Section 01About LMPM Housing

One Company,
Many Communities

Lone Mountain Property Management (LMPM) manages employee housing across a portfolio of world-class private clubs and businesses in the Big Sky and South Western area of Montana.

Whether you are joining Yellowstone Club, Spanish Peaks Mountain Club, Apres Cru, HHM, Moonlight Basin, or Crazy Mountain Ranch, your housing is administered through LMPM's centralized housing team and the StarRez Portal. This guide covers the policies, processes, and expectations that apply universally across all properties.

We’re glad you’re here. Employee Housing is here to support your season — clear communication and timely action help ensure a smooth experience for everyone.

Section 02Onboarding Process

Your Path to Move-In Day

Every step below must be completed before you can check in. Missing any single step is the most common cause of delays — read this carefully.

01

Receive your job offer

A signed offer letter from your employer’s HR team is required before a housing application can be initiated.

02

Pass your background check

Housing eligibility is contingent on a cleared background check. Your HR team will advise on timeline — you can continue with your application while you wait.

03

Register in the StarRez Portal

You will receive an email invitation from Human Resources. Register and complete your housing application through the StarRez Portal. Allow 3 business days for review. Business days are Monday – Friday.

04

Sign your lease agreement

Your seasonal lease is generated within the StarRez Portal once your application is approved. Sign it promptly — your bed space cannot be held without a signed lease.

05

Select your bed space

Bed spaces are first-come, first-served within the StarRez Portal. Some departments may be pre-assigned to specific locations based on operational needs.

06

Pay deposit & initial rent

Your security deposit is due within 5 days of selecting your bed space, or before arrival — whichever comes first. Initial rent must also be paid before check-in. See your StarRez Portal for amounts.

07

Schedule your move-in appointment

Book through the StarRez Portal with a minimum of 72 hours’ notice. Appointments cannot be scheduled sooner than 3 days before your work start date.

06

Transportation not provided

Residents who do not appear for their scheduled move-in appointment without communication within 24 hours will have their booking cancelled. A new bed space may be selected if and when plans are confirmed.

Application Eligibility Considerations

The items below are taken into consideration when reviewing applications.

Pay or vacate notices

3 or more in a prior season rotates an applicant to the end of the queue. Housing may be limited & is not guaranteed.

Non Sufficient Funds (NSF) notices

3 or more in a season rotates an applicant to the end of the queue. Housing may be limited & is not guaranteed.

Lease violations

2 or more in a season affects future approval. Severe violations may result in housing denial.

Early termination

Prior early contract terminations affect queue priority. Residents departing before seasonal cutoffs are added to the waitlist.

Section 08Universal Policies

Policies That Apply Everywhere

These policies apply across all LMPM-managed properties regardless of employer or location. Your signed lease agreement contains the full legal terms.

Quiet Hours

10:00PM – 7:00AM daily at all properties
Be considerate during non-quiet hours — residents work varying shifts
Repeated noise infractions may result in fines or eviction

Smoking & Vaping

Strictly prohibited inside all units and common areas
Permitted outdoors 25 feet from any door or open window
Violations: warning → $100 → $200–$300 → $500+ fines
Tampering with smoke detectors is prohibited

Pets & Animals

No pets permitted at any LMPM property
Support animals require pre-approval through OurPetPolicy
Letter of Disability & vaccination records required before move-in
Unauthorized pet fee: $500 per incident

Visitors

Visiting hours are 6:00AM – 10:00PM
Residents are responsible for all guest behavior
Unauthorized or unattended guests may result in fines or eviction

Substances & Weapons

All housing is drug-free — no illegal substances including marijuana
Alcohol permitted for residents 21+
No weapons, flammables, or explosives permitted at any time
Violations may result in immediate eviction and further consequences

Shared Space & Cleanliness

Common areas are your shared responsibility
Routine inspections are conducted at all properties
Trash must be deposited properly — never left outside compactors or bins
Cleaning fees and damage charges apply upon departure

Rent Payments

Rent paid biweekly or on the 1st/15th through the StarRez Portal / Zego. Please check your hiring company housing guide for specifics.
ACH payment is preferred — no processing fee
Late fees begin 3 calendar days after due date
Payroll deductions are not offered

Keys & Access

Keys and access codes are personal — sharing is prohibited
Lost or stolen keys must be reported immediately
Replacement and re-pin fees apply — see fee schedule
After-hours access: contact your Resident Assistant or the on-call security at your property

Employee housing is a shared living environment. Thoughtfulness about noise, shared spaces, and different schedules makes a genuine difference for everyone’s experience.

Section 11Who to Contact

We’re Here to Help

Use the right channel the first time — it gets your issue resolved faster for everyone.

Life-threatening emergencies
911

For any emergency involving personal safety or danger, call 911 immediately. Notify the LMPM Residents Team as soon as it is safe to do so.

After-hours emergencies
On-call support

Contact your property’s Resident Assistant or on-call security line. For leaks, flooding, no power or no heat, use the housing emergency line.

Maintenance requests
StarRez Portal

All non-emergency maintenance must be submitted through the StarRez Portal. Do not contact security for maintenance issues.

Section 12Packing List

Arrive Prepared

Furnishings and utilities are provided, but many personal items are not. Employee housing is a shared living environment — items that support sleep, organization, and personal space often make the biggest difference.

Government-issued IDs & documents

ID, lease copies, insurance information, prescriptions.

Bedding & towels

Not provided unless purchased through the StarRez Portal in advance. Twin XL is the most common size. Please refer to your company specific housing guide for more details.

Renter’s insurance

Personal belongings are not covered by LMPM. A basic renter’s policy is strongly recommended.

Warm-weather layering

Montana weather shifts dramatically. Bring a winter jacket, hat, gloves, and insulated boots regardless of season.

Section 13Quick Start & FAQ

Quick Start Guide

Designed to help you move into employee housing with minimal stress. Most delays, fees and issues occur when steps are missed.

Key timelines to know

Deposits due within 5 days of selecting a bed space, or before arrival — whichever comes first
Rent paid biweekly or on the 1st/15th through the StarRez Portal / Zego. Please check your hiring company housing guide for specifics.
Late fees begin 3 calendar days after the rent due date
Move-in appointments no sooner than 3 days before your work start date

Payments at a glance

Preferred method: ACH — no processing fee
ACH payments are not real-time and may be returned by your bank days later
Returned payments may result in Non Sufficient Funds (NSF) fees and a reinstated balance
Keep sufficient funds available for several days after paying. Payroll deductions are not offered

Shared living expectations

A roommate questionnaire identifies general preferences, but roommate compatibility is not guaranteed. Please be mindful of:

Quiet hours — and courtesy outside of them
Lights, TVs, alarms and devices in shared bedrooms
Differences in schedules, age and lifestyle
If concerns arise: speak respectfully with your roommate, contact your Resident Assistant, then reach out to LMPM if issues persist.

Common mistakes to avoid

Missing your move-in appointment
Submitting rent or deposits late
Assuming ACH payments clear immediately
Arriving without finalized housing steps
Expecting automatic roommate changes
Leaving without completing the move-out process
These issues may result in fees, cancelled bookings, or loss of housing eligibility.
